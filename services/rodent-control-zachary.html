Warning Signs of Rodent Activity in Your Home

  • Scratching or scurrying sounds in attics or walls — especially at night
  • Rodent droppings in cabinets, pantries or storage areas
  • Chewed wiring, insulation or structural materials
  • Gnaw marks around food packaging or wooden surfaces
  • Strong ammonia-like odors in enclosed spaces
  • Nesting material (shredded paper, fabric or insulation) in hidden areas
  • Grease marks along walls where rodents travel regularly

Rodent Control FAQ — Zachary Louisiana

Are rodents common in Zachary Louisiana?
Yes. Zachary's agricultural surroundings and wooded residential areas create conditions that regularly attract rats and mice. Rodent activity typically increases in fall as temperatures drop and animals seek shelter indoors.
What attracts rodents to homes in Zachary?
Accessible food sources, cluttered garages or storage areas, dense landscaping near the foundation and small entry gaps around rooflines or utility penetrations are the most common attractants. Rural proximity to fields significantly increases rodent pressure.
How do rodents get inside houses?
Mice can enter through gaps as small as a quarter inch. Rats need only about half an inch. Common entry points include gaps around rooflines, soffit vents, garage doors, utility pipes and HVAC penetrations.
What should I do if I hear scratching in my attic?
Scratching sounds in attics or walls, especially at night, are strong indicators of rodent or wildlife activity. Contact a pest professional promptly — the longer you wait, the larger the infestation can become.
Do rodents carry diseases?
Yes. Rodents can transmit bacteria through droppings, urine and contact with contaminated surfaces. This is one of the primary reasons infestations should be addressed quickly.
