Zachary Louisiana Has High Termite Risk

Zachary sits in one of the most termite-active regions in the entire country. Subterranean termites thrive in Louisiana's warm soil, high moisture levels and consistent humidity — remaining active year-round and silently damaging structural wood inside walls, floors and crawlspaces.

Homes in Zachary with wooded lots, older construction or high soil moisture near the foundation are at particularly elevated risk. Annual inspections are strongly recommended for all homeowners in the area.

Common Signs of Termite Activity in Zachary Homes

  • Mud tubes along foundation walls or piers
  • Discarded termite wings near windows and doors
  • Hollow-sounding or soft wood in floors and walls
  • Swarming termites, especially in spring
  • Bubbling or blistering paint on walls
  • Visible damage to wood structures in crawlspaces
