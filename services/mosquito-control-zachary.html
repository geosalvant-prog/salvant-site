Mosquito Control FAQ — Zachary Louisiana

How often should mosquito spraying be done in Zachary?
Most homeowners see the best results with treatments every 3–4 weeks during peak mosquito season (approximately March through November in Zachary). This schedule keeps mosquito populations consistently reduced throughout the season.
Will mosquito control completely eliminate mosquitoes?
No treatment eliminates every mosquito — new ones can always migrate from surrounding areas. However, professional seasonal treatments significantly reduce populations and make outdoor spaces much more enjoyable.
Can you treat for a one-time outdoor event?
Yes. We offer one-time treatments for parties, cookouts and outdoor events in addition to our recurring seasonal programs.
What areas of the yard are treated?
Treatment focuses on shaded vegetation, shrubs, fence lines, drainage areas and other areas where mosquitoes rest during the day.
What can homeowners do between treatments?
Eliminating standing water is the single most effective step. Empty containers, clean gutters and address any low spots that hold water after rain to reduce mosquito breeding between visits.
