Common Nuisance Wildlife in Zachary

  • Raccoons — frequently found in attics and crawlspaces
  • Squirrels — often enter through roofline gaps and soffit areas
  • Opossums — may shelter under structures or in attics
  • Bats — can roost in attic spaces through very small openings
  • Rodents (rats and mice) — entry through gaps in rooflines and utility areas

Signs of Wildlife Activity in Your Home

  • Scratching, thumping or movement sounds in attic or walls
  • Noises most pronounced at night or early morning
  • Damaged or displaced roof vents, soffits or fascia boards
  • Animal droppings or nesting material in attic spaces
  • Strong ammonia or musky odors from attic or enclosed areas
  • Visible entry holes or damaged areas around the roofline

Wildlife Removal FAQ — Zachary Louisiana

What wildlife commonly enters homes in Zachary?
Raccoons, squirrels, opossums and bats are among the most common nuisance wildlife species that enter homes in the Zachary area. Zachary's wooded lots and proximity to rural land increase the likelihood of wildlife encounters compared to denser urban areas.
What should I do if I hear animals in my attic?
Don't ignore it. Contact a pest professional promptly. Animals cause real damage to insulation, ductwork and wiring. The sooner they are removed and entry points sealed, the less damage your home sustains.
Can wildlife damage my home?
Yes. Raccoons and squirrels commonly tear insulation, chew wiring and damage ductwork. Bat colonies leave droppings that can be substantial and create health concerns. All of these issues become more expensive the longer they go unaddressed.
Do you remove dead animals?
Yes. Salvant Environmental Services provides dead animal removal from walls, attics, crawlspaces and under structures — locating and removing the source of odor rather than simply treating the symptom.
How do animals get into attics?
Wildlife most commonly enter through gaps in rooflines, damaged soffit panels, uncapped roof vents and openings around fascia boards. Squirrels can chew through wood and plastic vents. Raccoons are strong enough to pull back loose soffit material.
